# Project Description  

  

Join our Development Centre in Bucharest and become a member of an open-minded, progressive and professional team.  

  

In this role you will be working on projects for one of Luxoft's world-famous clients.  

  

You will have a chance to grow your technical and soft skills and build a thorough expertise of the client's industry.  

  

In addition to an attractive salary and benefits package, Luxoft will invest in professional training and career development.  

  

---  

  

# Role Summary  

  

- Work in a scaled Agile environment.  

- Be part of a global and diverse team.  

- Contribute to all stages of the software development lifecycle.  

- Participate in peer reviews of solution designs and code.  

- Maintain high standards of software quality through established engineering practices.  

- Use frameworks such as Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) and LangGraph to build robust, controllable and observable agentic architectures.  

- Assist in designing LLM-powered agents and multi-agent workflows.  

- Lead implementation, deployment and testing of multi-agent systems.  

- Mentor junior engineers on LLM engineering and agentic systems.  

- Drive technical discussions regarding AI architecture and framework adoption.  

- Identify and address technical debt within AI systems.  

- Represent the team in cross-functional technical discussions.
